# a11y: muted text at 4.5:1, and `--fg-4` for what isn't read

Date: 2026-11-23. Task: `a11y` (survey-3 task 38). Code: `src/styles/themes.css`, `src/themes.ts` (`CONTRAST_CHECKS`), `src/userThemes.ts`, `src/styles/app.css`. The themes-and-cosmetics task (`docs/plans/completed/themes-and-cosmetics.md`) held the original eight themes to fewer checks than the 31 it added; this takes that exemption away.

## Context
`--fg-3` (muted text: sizes, dates, hints, placeholders) was 2.7–3.4:1 in the original eight themes, and `--fg-2` was under 4.5:1 on some surfaces. WCAG AA asks 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for the parts of controls, and exempts disabled controls and decoration. `--fg-3` was used for all three: text, disabled-looking marks and decoration.

## Chosen
**Text tokens reach 4.5:1 on every surface they're drawn on, in all 39 themes.**
- `--fg-2` and `--fg-3` are checked on `--bg-0` (the file list), `--bg-1` (panels) and `--bg-2` (hover, inputs). The exemption for the original eight is gone.
- Each color kept its hue and saturation; only its lightness moved, and only as far as 4.5:1 needs (the lead's rule), so the themes stay themselves. Tokyo Night's `--fg-3` went from `#565f89` to `#8089af`, for example.
- Where that put `--fg-3` past `--fg-2`, `--fg-2` moved by the same rule, so the order primary → secondary → muted holds on each surface (`themes.test.ts` checks it).
- Solarized Dark's accent moved a shade lighter (`#d33682` → `#d53e87`) to reach 3:1 as a focus ring, with a dark `--on-accent` for text on it.

**`--fg-4` is the color for what isn't read:** separators, placeholder dashes, decorative glyphs, the unfocused pane's tab mark, an off permission cell. In each original theme it's the old `--fg-3`, so those marks look as they did, and nothing is asked of its contrast. Raising them with the text would have made disabled-looking things look enabled.
- A selected row's muted cells take the row's own text color: `--fg-3` on `--bg-sel` can't reach 4.5:1 in several themes whatever its lightness.
- The focus ring on a selected row is `--fg-0`, checked at 3:1 on `--bg-sel`; elsewhere it's the accent, checked at 3:1 on the three surfaces.

**Saved custom themes still load.** A theme saved before `--fg-4` has no value for it and takes its own `--fg-3` (`checkTheme`). Nothing else about the format changed. The theme editor's contrast report uses the same `CONTRAST_CHECKS`, so a custom theme is told about muted text under 4.5:1 too; it's a report, and the theme still saves.

## What it costs
- The original eight look slightly different: secondary and muted text is lighter (darker in the light ones). The CHANGELOG's themes entry said they look as they did; it needs a Changed line.
- In seven themes with a narrow range between text and background (Solarized Dark and Light, Catppuccin Latte, Rosé Pine Dawn, Tokyo Night Day, Everforest Light, Kanagawa Lotus), primary, secondary and muted text now sit close together, all near 4.5:1. Keeping more of a step between them would have meant moving hue or saturation, or the backgrounds.
- Borders drawn with `--fg-3` are a little stronger.

## Not covered
- Colored text (the red of an error, git's letters) is still held to 3:1, as before.
- A danger menu item's red on the hover color isn't checked.
- `forced-colors` and reduced motion: the rules this task added are covered (the tab close button, the selected row's ring, the header and tab focus outlines; nothing new animates). The existing `[data-motion]` switch already follows `prefers-reduced-motion`.
